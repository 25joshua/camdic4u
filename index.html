About / 소개

Camdic4U는 카메라로 찍은 글자 속 단어를 바로 사전처럼 조회하고, 뜻을 저장해 복습까지 이어가는 모바일 학습 앱입니다.

Camdic4U is a mobile app that turns any photographed text into a personal dictionary. Look up words in context, save meanings to your vocabulary, and review them with spaced repetition.

Features / 주요 기능

📷

OCR text recognition

Photograph signs, menus, books, or documents — text is recognized on-device.

📖

Multilingual dictionary

Look up words in English, Chinese, Japanese, Spanish, French, German, Portuguese, Hindi, Indonesian, and Vietnamese.

🗣

Vocabulary & SRS review

Save words to folders and review them with spaced repetition scheduling.

🔊

Text-to-speech & pronunciation

Hear words and sentences aloud; practice pronunciation with speech recognition.
